# Competitor dossier template

`<state>/competitors/<slug>.md`. A fact without a URL, an access date and a quote doesn't go in.

```markdown
# <Competitor> — dossier — refreshed <YYYY-MM-DD>

Domains: <primary>, <docs>, <app>, <regional mirrors>    ← also add these to the panel's competitors[].domains
Products / editions in scope: <…>

## Positioning (their words)
- Homepage headline: "<quote>" — <url>, <date>
- Paid search claims: "<ad headline>" ×N ads — Google Ads Transparency via <endpoint>, <date>

## Pricing (as of <date>)
| plan | price | billing | key limits | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Capabilities relevant to our buyers
| criterion | their documented behavior | quote | source | date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <criterion> | yes / no / partial / not documented | "<exact words>" | <url> | <date> |

## Where they are genuinely strong
- <honest list; this feeds the "choose them if" section>

## User-reported pain (patterns, with counts)
- <theme>: N of M reviews / threads, <period> — sources: <trustpilot url>, <reddit search terms>

## What AI engines say about them
- <engine>: "<summary>" — cites <domains> — run <date>
- Errors engines make about them (do not repeat): <…>

## Pages that rely on this dossier
- <site path> (rows: <criteria>)
```

Refresh it when the watcher reports a change, before any page that uses it is edited, and at least
quarterly.
